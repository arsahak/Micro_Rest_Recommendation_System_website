1 Signing in

Go to /login. You'll need two things from your researcher:

  • Participant ID — P01 through P10. This anonymous ID stands in for your name everywhere in the app.
  • PIN — if this is your first time signing in, type any PIN you like; it becomes your permanent PIN for the study from that point on. If you've signed in before, use the same PIN.

You're taken to My Dashboard (/user) on success.

2 Turning on notifications (optional, but recommended)

On the dashboard, you'll see one of three states:

  • "🔔 Enable Notifications" — click it and allow the browser's permission prompt. This lets check-in reminders and risk alerts show up as OS-level pop-ups, not just inside the app.
  • "🔔 Notifications on" — already enabled, nothing to do.
  • "🔕 Notifications blocked" — you (or your browser) blocked this at some point. Pop-ups won't work until you re-allow them: click the lock/info icon next to your browser's address bar → Notifications → Allow → reload the page. The dashboard explains this same fix if you see this state.

Notifications are always silent (no sound) — just a visual pop-up.

3 Starting a Work Session

Under Current Work Session, click Start Work Session. This begins tracking:

  • Session time — a live clock, how long the session's been running.
  • Sitting / Screen — minutes accumulated while the tab is open and visible, reset to zero every time you complete a rest.
  • Check-ins — how many you've submitted this session.

You need an active session for check-in reminders to work — without one, the app has nothing to measure "time since your last break" against.

4 Getting a check-in reminder

While a session is active, the app quietly re-checks every ~10 minutes (and once immediately when the page loads) whether a check-in is due. It becomes due if any of these are true:

  • Enough time has passed since your last check-in (this interval adjusts itself — see below).
  • You've been sitting 60+ minutes.
  • Your screen's been on 60+ minutes.
  • 90+ minutes have passed with no rest taken.

When due, you'll see an amber Due card with the reason (e.g. rest_threshold_elapsed), a Check in button, and a Snooze button. This reminder repeats every ~10 minutes for as long as it stays outstanding — it won't stop nagging just because you dismissed the OS pop-up once.

Why the interval changes: after each check-in, the app sets your next reminder based on how that check-in went — Low risk buys you 60 minutes before the next nudge, Medium 30, High 15. If you skip or snooze a suggested rest, the next reminder is capped at 15 minutes regardless of your risk level.

6 Reading your result

You're taken straight to your result page, showing:

  • Risk level — Low Medium High, plus the numeric score (0–100).
  • Dominant issue — whichever factor scored highest (e.g. "Long Sitting," "Visual Fatigue").
  • Recommended micro-rest — only shown for Medium/High (e.g. a 60-second eye break, a posture stretch, a short walk). Low risk gets no rest suggestion — you're doing fine, keep going.
  • Risk Score Breakdown — a per-factor readout so you can see exactly what drove the result.
  • Reading Details — your current HR, HR deviation from baseline, the check-in ID, and the dominant issue.

If risk is Medium or High, you'll also get a real-time pop-up notification with the result (if notifications are enabled) — Low risk intentionally gets no pop-up, since there's nothing to act on.

7 Doing the micro-rest and giving feedback

If a micro-rest was suggested, click Start Micro-Rest →, actually do the suggested action, then rate it:

  • Did you complete it?
  • How useful was it? (1–5)
  • Was the timing appropriate? (1–5)
  • How disruptive was it to your work? (1–5)
  • Did you feel recovered afterward? (1–5)

This feeds back into your session: completing a rest resets your sitting/screen counters to zero and records when you last rested. Skipping it (or saying you still feel drained) tightens your next check-in interval to 15 minutes, same as a High-risk result would.

You can also click Skip & Give Feedback straight from the result page if you don't want to do the rest right now, or ← New Check-in to go straight to another check-in.

8 The notification bell 🔔

The bell in the navbar (top right, next to your name) shows your own recent notifications — never anyone else's. Three kinds, each with its own badge:

BadgeMeaningLinks to

It refreshes automatically every 10 seconds. The unread count clears when you open it.

9 Viewing your history

Go to My History (/history). Two tabs:

  • Check-ins — every check-in you've submitted, with risk level, score, dominant issue, and links to view the full result or add feedback. Filter by risk level (All / High / Medium / Low) using the chips above the table.
  • Baseline Entries — your recorded baseline readings from Phase 1.

10 Baseline

If you haven't completed your baseline yet, the dashboard shows an amber "Baseline not set up yet" card — go to Baseline (/baseline) and log a few readings while resting normally (Before Work, After Work, etc.). The app averages these into your personal baseline HR, which every future check-in's HR deviation is measured against.

11 Ending your session / signing out

  • End Session (in the Current Work Session card) — stops time tracking. Do this when you're done for the day; start a new one next time you begin work.
  • Sign out (top right) — logs you out. Your data isn't affected; sign back in any time with the same Participant ID and PIN.

12 Troubleshooting

"Invalid participant ID or PIN" — either you mistyped, or your account doesn't exist yet (ask your researcher to confirm your ID is set up). This is not the same as a forgotten PIN — there's no PIN reset; contact your researcher if you're genuinely locked out.

No pop-up notifications, ever — check the dashboard's notification indicator. If it says blocked, follow the on-screen fix (browser address bar → lock/info icon → Notifications → Allow → reload). If it says "Notifications on" and you still see nothing, the in-app bell will still have the record — pop-ups and the bell are independent, so you haven't missed anything permanently.

"Backend unreachable" message anywhere — the API server isn't running or isn't reachable. This is an infrastructure issue, not something fixable from the participant side; let your researcher know.

A check-in reminder won't stop — that's intentional. It keeps repeating every ~10 minutes until you either check in or end the session. Snoozing dismisses the current pop-up but doesn't stop the underlying timer from evaluating again next cycle.
